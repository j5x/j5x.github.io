About Me

I'm a 20-year-old game developer and digital creator from Edam-Volendam, Netherlands, driven by a deep fascination with emotion, atmosphere, and storytelling through technology. My journey began with a love for games, not just as entertainment, but as vessels for meaning and connection. That fascination pushed me to explore Unity and Unreal Engine, where I’ve been learning to turn imagination into interactive experiences that resonate with people.

Beyond development, I channel my creativity through animation and editing using visuals, rhythm, and motion to evoke feeling. I also do boxing, which keeps me grounded and teaches me discipline, focus, and resilience.

Right now, I’m looking for a place in a creative and growth-oriented environment, a place that values experimentation, workflow, and emotional storytelling through design. My goal is to keep improving, keep creating, and contribute to projects that move people the way games once moved me.
