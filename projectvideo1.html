Cinematic Works

Classroom Compositing

Project Overview

This project demonstrates a hybrid 2D/3D compositing workflow that integrates Spine 2D character animation, Blender lighting and camera compositing, EmberGen volumetric simulations, and After Effects post-production. The focus is on achieving a cohesive cinematic look by blending 2D animation with 3D lighting and particle effects.

Role

Animator, Compositor & Editor

Duration

1-2 weeks

Client

Personal Project

Tools Used

Blender Spine2D Photoshop After Effects

Concept & Planning

The project began with extensive research into japanese aesthetics and their school environments. I created mood boards and reference collections to establish the visual direction, focusing on creating a believable yet stylized world with strong atmospheric lighting.

Blender Classroom and Model

Workflow

Using Photoshop, I redrew and seperated the character into segments for animation. Special attention was given to eyes and hair to add realism. The classroom was a model i found online, i then tweaked and changed parts to make it fit the composit better..

Final rendered scene with all effects applied

Technical Challenges

One of the biggest challenges was fixing the lighting in the classroom, because the lightrays didnt want to render through the class, so i had to remodel the side i wanted to use for the render.
