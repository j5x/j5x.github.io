3D Modeling & VFX

Cyberpunk Environment Design

Project Overview

A complete cyberpunk city environment featuring neon-lit streets, holographic advertisements, and atmospheric effects. This project showcases advanced 3D modeling techniques combined with particle effects and post-processing.

Role

Lead 3D Artist & VFX Designer

Duration

6 weeks

Client

Personal Project

Tools Used

Blender Unreal Engine Substance Painter Houdini

Concept & Planning

The project began with extensive research into cyberpunk aesthetics and urban environments. I created mood boards and reference collections to establish the visual direction, focusing on creating a believable yet stylized world with strong atmospheric lighting.

3D Modeling Process

Texturing & Materials

Using Substance Painter, I created detailed PBR materials for all assets. Special attention was given to weathering and wear patterns to add realism. The metallic surfaces needed to reflect the neon lights realistically while maintaining good performance.

VFX Breakdown

Particle systems and volumetric lighting setup

Final rendered scene with all effects applied

Technical Challenges

One of the biggest challenges was optimizing the scene for real-time rendering while maintaining visual fidelity. I implemented LOD systems for distant buildings and optimized the particle effects to run smoothly at 60fps.
